Attending Physician FAQs

I've been an attending for 5 years. Do I still qualify?

Absolutely! Our attending physician program is available to physicians at any stage of their career. Many established physicians use our program to upgrade homes or purchase investment properties.

Can I buy an investment property?

Yes! Unlike traditional physician loan programs, we offer investment property financing for attending physicians with as little as 15% down and no PMI.

What's the maximum loan amount?

We offer physician mortgages up to $2.5 million for qualifying attending physicians. Higher amounts may be available on a case-by-case basis.

Are rates different for attendings vs. residents?

Established attending physicians with stable income history may qualify for slightly better rates compared to residents, reflecting the lower risk profile.

Can I refinance my existing home with this program?

Yes! We offer rate-and-term refinancing for physicians looking to lower their rate or change loan terms. Cash-out refinancing is also available depending on your equity position.
