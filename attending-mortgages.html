A "qualified borrower" is typically a medical resident, fellow or attending physician with a signed contract for employment.

Including:

  • Physicians (MD, DO, DP)
  • Medical Residents or Fellows in MD, DO programs
  • Dentists (DDS, DMD)

What if I'm self-employed?

We offer specialty underwriting for 1099 and contract employees. We understand that many physicians work as independent contractors or have complex income structures.

Can I refinance my current home?

Yes! We offer refinancing options including cash-out refinancing for attending physicians looking to access equity or lower their rate.
