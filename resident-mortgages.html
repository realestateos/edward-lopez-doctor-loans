Resident Mortgage FAQs

Can I qualify with my resident salary?

Yes! We can qualify you based on your future attending salary with a signed employment contract. Many residents close on homes before they ever receive their first attending paycheck.

What if I don't have a job offer yet?

We can still pre-qualify you based on typical attending salaries for your specialty and location. Once you have a signed contract, we can update your pre-approval with the exact numbers.

How much can I borrow?

Loan amounts up to $2 million are available. Your specific amount depends on your future income, other debts, and the loan program you choose.

Do I need a down payment?

No! We offer 100% financing for qualified residents. However, if you do have savings for a down payment, it can improve your rate and reduce your monthly payment.

What about my $300K in student loans?

If your student loans are deferred for more than 12 months from closing, they are completely excluded from your debt-to-income ratio. This is one of the biggest advantages of physician loans.
