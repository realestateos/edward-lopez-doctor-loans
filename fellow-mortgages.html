Mortgages for Physicians in Fellowship

As a fellow, once you've completed your residency and chosen a specialty you have a unique borrower profile from the mortgage lender's perspective. Whether you have to move across the country, or you're in limbo while your contract is finalized — we understand the hurdles you face.

Future Income Consideration

A mortgage specifically designed for you

Our physician mortgage programs were developed for your unique situation as a fellow and borrower profile. Our unique mortgage products can take your future income into consideration just by using your contract.

Let's say you've gotten a contract with an employment start date of 60 days from now — we'll work with you to leverage that future income as part of your home loan application to increase your odds of approval.
