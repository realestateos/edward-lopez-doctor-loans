Fellow Mortgage FAQs

Can I buy a home in a different city than my fellowship?

Yes! If you have a signed employment contract for your attending position, we can help you buy in that city even while you're still completing fellowship elsewhere.

When should I start the process?

Ideally 3-6 months before you want to close. This gives you time to get pre-approved, shop for homes, and close before your move date.

What if I don't have my contract yet?

We can pre-qualify you based on typical salaries for your specialty in your target area. Once you have your contract, we can update your approval.

Do high-earning specialties get better rates?

While rates are generally consistent, high-earning specialties may qualify for higher loan amounts and more flexible terms due to lower risk profiles.
